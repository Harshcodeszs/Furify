Tips For Better Dog Breed Identification

Curious about your dog's breed? Use these simple tips to help our AI identify your dog more accurately.

Clear, Well-Lit Photos

Use bright, clear photos. Avoid dark or blurry images.

Face-Forward

Show your dog's face clearly from the front.

High Quality

Use sharp photos that show fur, ears, and facial features.

Full Body

Include your dog's entire body to show its shape and size.

Visible Features

Make sure the eyes, nose, ears, and body are easy to see.

Simple Background

Use a clean background without people or other animals.

Mixed-breed dogs can have traits from different breeds, so photo identification may not always be exact. Use clear photos for better results and consider DNA testing for confirmation.
