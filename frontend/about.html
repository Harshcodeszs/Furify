About Furify

Welcome to Furify, your simple and fun AI-powered dog breed identifier! Upload a photo of your dog and let Furify analyze its visible features to find the most likely breed match. Whether you're curious about your dog's breed or simply want to learn more about your furry friend, Furify makes it quick and easy.

How It Works

Furify uses AI to analyze your dog's photo and compare its features with different dog breeds. In just a few seconds, you'll receive a breed prediction along with useful information about the result. All you need to do is:

  • Upload a clear photo of your dog.
  • Let Furify analyze the visible features.
  • Get your results with the most likely breed matches and breed information.
